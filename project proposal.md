

Intelligent E‑Learning Platform with Automated Face Recognition–Based Attendance Monitoring

PROJECT PROPOSAL DOCUMENT




1. Introduction / Background
The expansion of digital education has transformed how institutions deliver learning materials, conduct classes, and interact with students. E-learning platforms such as Google Classroom, Moodle, and Zoom are widely used, but their attendance verification systems remain unreliable. Most platforms depend on outdated attendance methods-manual roll calls, Google Forms, or simple login timestamps-none of which confirm whether the student is physically present or actively participating.
This gap creates opportunities for impersonation, absenteeism, and misuse of online classes. A student may log in, disable their camera, and be marked present without attending the session. Others may ask friends or family to attend on their behalf.
To solve this issue, this project proposes developing an intelligent e-learning platform equipped with a real-time face recognition-based attendance system. By blending e-learning functionalities with artificial intelligence technologies, the platform aims to verify identities automatically, ensure fair attendance recording, reduce administrative burden, and improve accountability in online classrooms.
    1. Problem Statement
Despite the rapid adoption of e-learning, current platforms fail to verify student identity during online classes effectively. This weakness results in:
    • Inaccurate attendance records.
    • High chances of impersonation and fraudulent participation.
    • Lack of real-time identity verification.
    • Reduced teacher control over class participation.
    • Low academic integrity and poor learning outcomes.
Research Questions
    1. How can AI-driven face recognition be integrated into an e-learning platform to automate identity verification?
    2. How accurate is automated face recognition attendance compared to traditional methods?
    3. What system architecture best supports real-time face recognition during online learning sessions?
3. Objectives
3.1 General Objective
To design and develop an intelligent e-learning platform that delivers lectures, manages courses, and automatically verifies and records student attendance using AI-powered face recognition technology.
3.2 Specific Objectives
    • Develop a user-friendly web-based e-learning platform with classroom, course, and content management features.
    • Implement real-time face detection and face recognition modules using deep learning.
    • Automate attendance logging based on verified identity recognition.
    • Provide a teacher dashboard for live attendance monitoring and reporting.
    • Evaluate the system’s accuracy, usability, and performance under different conditions.
4. Scope of the Project and Limitations
Scope
The proposed system will include:
    • Secure login and student authentication.
    • Course and class management (upload materials, create sessions).
    • Live video classroom using Live Kit or Jitsi.
    • Automated face recognition to identify and mark students present.
    • Attendance reports for instructors.
    • AI-based monitoring integrated into the live stream.
Limitations
    • Face recognition accuracy may be affected by lighting, camera quality, and poor internet connection.
    • Large classes may require higher server processing power for real-time video frame analysis.
    • The system works only when the student’s camera is active.
    • Integration with institutional student databases may require additional configuration.

6. Significance and Beneficiaries
Significance
This project provides a significant improvement to online learning by ensuring:
    • Genuine student presence through verified attendance.
    • Reduced administrative workload for teachers.
    • Higher academic integrity and transparency.
    • Reliable participation data for institutional decisions.
    • Enhanced monitoring of student engagement.
Beneficiaries
    • Students: Fair attendance system, reduced impersonation, and improved participation accountability.
    • Teachers: Automated attendance, real-time verification, and reduced classroom management overhead.
    • Institutions: Improved reporting accuracy, academic credibility, and insights into student engagement patterns.
